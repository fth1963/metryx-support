English

Metryx is an app for tracking meter readings for electricity, water, gas, heating and other resources. The app lets you enter readings quickly, view clear charts and see estimated annual consumption based on your history.

If you need help, have found a bug, or would like to suggest a new feature, you can contact the developer by e-mail:

a4@proton.me

When you write, please include:

  • Which device and iOS version you are using
  • The app version (shown in the app settings or App Store)
  • A short description of the problem, and if possible a screenshot

The developer aims to respond to support requests within 2–3 working days.
